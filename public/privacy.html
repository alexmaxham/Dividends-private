Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Dividends is a personal dividend portfolio tracker. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The short version: your data is used only to show you your own portfolio. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to trade or move money.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password. Your password goes directly to Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it only in hashed form; the app itself never stores it.
  • Brokerage data, when you link an account: through Plaid, the app receives read-only information about your investment accounts: holdings (securities, quantities, cost basis and prices), cash balances, and investment transactions such as dividends, buys and sells, plus the name of your brokerage.
  • Information you enter: holdings, dividends and CSV files you add by hand, and your settings, such as an income goal and tax rate.
  • Preferences on your device: theme, glass appearance and privacy mode are saved in your browser's local storage and stay on that device.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

How we use Plaid

Linking a brokerage uses Plaid Inc. ("Plaid"). You sign in to your brokerage in Plaid's own window, so this app never receives your brokerage username or password. By linking an account, you allow Plaid to collect data from your brokerage and share it with this app, as described in Plaid's End User Privacy Policy. The access Plaid provides is read-only: the app cannot place trades, transfer funds or change anything in your brokerage account.

How we use your information

  • To show you your holdings, dividend income, upcoming payouts, insights and projections.
  • To refresh your linked accounts automatically once a day, and when you choose Sync now.
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure, and send account emails such as email verification and password resets.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than running the app for you.

Where your information is stored

Your account and portfolio are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States, and processed by Google Cloud Functions. The credential that lets the app read your linked accounts (a Plaid access token) is stored on the server only and is never sent to your browser. The app's Plaid keys are kept in Google Secret Manager.

Who can see your information

Only you. Each account can read only its own data, enforced by the database's security rules. Your information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app: Google (hosting, sign-in and storage) and Plaid (connecting brokerages). We may disclose information if required by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to the app's operator. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Unlink a brokerage any time in Settings. This revokes the app's access at Plaid immediately. You can also manage connections at my.plaid.com.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account. This revokes every linked brokerage at Plaid and permanently deletes your portfolio, history, settings and sign-in.
  • Download a copy of your data any time with Settings → Data → Back up.

Retention

We keep your information while your account exists. When you unlink a brokerage, previously synced history stays in your account until you delete it or your account. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the database right away.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
